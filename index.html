What Makes The Loft Special

The Loft has been thoughtfully designed for comfort and independence — perfect for a peaceful stay with everything you need close at hand. Wake up to tranquil views of wooded gardens and rolling Surrey countryside. Enjoy complete privacy with your own front door, kitchen, bathroom, and living space.

Self-Contained Living

Complete independence with your own kitchen and bathroom on the ground floor. Everything you need for a comfortable stay.

Countryside Views

Wake up to beautiful views of the Surrey countryside from your bright and airy loft accommodation.

Cozy Woodburner

Stay warm and create a perfect ambiance during cooler months with our traditional woodburner fireplace.

Recently Refurbished

Enjoy modern amenities and contemporary design throughout this thoughtfully updated space.

Perfect for Two

Ideal accommodation for couples or friends seeking a peaceful retreat in beautiful Surrey.

Excellent Transport Links
